## Context

Livemint is part of HT Digital Streams, and its platforms serve **20M+ monthly active users**. The platform was built on VanillaJS.

## My role

As Tech Lead, I spearheaded the **end-to-end modernization** of the platform.

## What changed

- **Migrated from VanillaJS to Next.js**, including server-side rendering.
- **Architected domain-driven Micro Frontends**, using CDN path routing.
- **Built for high traffic**, including the surges that come with elections and budgets.

## Outcome

- **20% improvement** in page load speeds.
- **Up to 35% improvement** in LCP and INP.
- **15% reduction** in bounce rates.
